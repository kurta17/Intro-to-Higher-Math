Hard-won

Delimiters: $…$ inline, $$…$$ display.

In HTML you must escape < as &lt; — even inside maths.

Drop the doctype and every formula silently fails (quirks mode). driver.mjs check catches it.

Every claim on a slide carries a check block — this slide included. See the /lecture-content skill.
